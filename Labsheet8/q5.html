1. ARIA Landmarks & Keyboard Focus Control

Hit the Tab key on your keyboard to observe clean, high-visibility focus indicators around interactive elements.

role="banner"

Identifies site-wide header containing branding, global actions, and primary navigation.

role="navigation"

Provides explicit navigation landmark with aria-label="Main Navigation" for assistive technologies.

role="main"

Marks unique primary content directly reachable via the skip-link shortcut.

aria-live="polite"

Announces status updates and dynamic validation alerts without interrupting speech synthesis.
