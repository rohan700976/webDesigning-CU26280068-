Botanica Eco-Cafe & Artisan Bakery

Comprehensive architectural specification, target audience profiling, and wireframe schematics designed for a premier sustainable culinary venue located near COER University, Roorkee.

1. Project Specification & Scope

Client Profile

Botanica Cafe & Bakery is an eco-conscious culinary brand specializing in 100% organic sourdough, slow-fermented pastries, and direct-trade single-origin espresso.

Target Audience

University students, faculty, remote workers, eco-advocates, and gourmet pastry enthusiasts looking for healthy, artisanal refreshments in an inspiring green ambiance.

Brand Positioning

Eco-elegance, ethical transparency, zero single-use plastics, compostable packaging, and celebration of botanical ingredients with farm-to-table freshness.

Core Deliverables

Semantic HTML5 structure, mobile-first responsive layout, WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility, hero carousel, reservation engine, and interactive Google Maps.
